# 2.3 Configurar clientes Eureka

hr-worker e hr-payroll viraram clientes do Eureka: dependência `spring-cloud-starter-netflix-eureka-client` adicionada nos dois, `@EnableEurekaClient` nas classes principais, e `eureka.client.service-url.defaultZone=http://localhost:8761/eureka` apontando pro hr-eureka-server.

O que existia antes e não é mais necessário: o `@RibbonClient(name = "hr-worker")` no hr-payroll e a lista fixa `hr-worker.ribbon.listOfServers=localhost:8001,localhost:8002`. Antes o Ribbon precisava saber manualmente onde cada instância do hr-worker estava rodando. Com o Eureka, o hr-worker se registra sozinho ao subir, e o Ribbon busca a lista de instâncias disponíveis direto do Eureka, dinamicamente, sem precisar de config fixa.

Resultado já visível: `WorkerFeignClient` (`@FeignClient(name = "hr-worker", path="/workers")`) já descobre o hr-worker só pelo nome lógico "hr-worker", sem porta nem endereço nenhum declarado no código do hr-payroll. Quem resolve isso em tempo real é o Eureka.

As portas continuam fixas por enquanto (hr-worker em 8001, hr-payroll em 8101). Porta aleatória/automática fica pra próxima aula.

---

#### English

hr-worker and hr-payroll became Eureka clients: `spring-cloud-starter-netflix-eureka-client` dependency added to both, `@EnableEurekaClient` on the main classes, and `eureka.client.service-url.defaultZone=http://localhost:8761/eureka` pointing to hr-eureka-server.

What existed before and is no longer needed: the `@RibbonClient(name = "hr-worker")` in hr-payroll and the fixed list `hr-worker.ribbon.listOfServers=localhost:8001,localhost:8002`. Before, Ribbon needed to be told manually where each hr-worker instance was running. With Eureka, hr-worker registers itself on startup, and Ribbon fetches the list of available instances directly from Eureka, dynamically, without needing fixed config.

Already visible result: `WorkerFeignClient` (`@FeignClient(name = "hr-worker", path="/workers")`) already discovers hr-worker just by its logical name "hr-worker", with no port or address declared anywhere in hr-payroll's code. Eureka resolves that at runtime.

Ports are still fixed for now (hr-worker on 8001, hr-payroll on 8101). Random/automatic ports come in the next lesson.
